About This System

This Loan Approval Risk Analysis System uses machine learning algorithms to assess credit risk and predict default probability. The system analyzes multiple financial indicators including credit score, income, debt levels, employment stability, and credit history to provide a comprehensive risk assessment.

Risk Scoring Methodology

Low Risk (0-30): Strong financial profile with excellent credit history and stable income.
Medium Risk (31-70): Acceptable financial profile with some minor concerns or limited credit history.
High Risk (71-100): Significant financial concerns, poor credit history, or high debt-to-income ratio.

Key Factors Analyzed

  • Credit Score: Higher scores indicate better creditworthiness
  • Debt-to-Income Ratio: Lower ratios are preferred (target < 43%)
  • Employment Stability: Longer tenure reduces default risk
  • Payment History: Number of late payments is a key predictor
  • Income Level: Adequate income to support loan repayment
  • Loan-to-Value Ratio: Property-based loans have lower default rates
